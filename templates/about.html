Who We Are

From Humble Beginnings to a Flourishing Brand
Gull Rukh was born during the challenging times of COVID, starting as a small venture on Facebook. Like any journey, we faced our share of ups and downs, but with the right support and unwavering dedication, we not only survived—we thrived. Today, Gull Rukh stands as a testament to resilience, passion, and the love of our loyal customers.

Our Mission

A Woman’s Pride
At Gull Rukh, we believe that every woman should wear what she loves with pride and confidence. Our brand is more than just fashion—it’s about embracing individuality and celebrating the beauty of Pakistani culture through vibrant colors, diverse styles, and elegant designs.

With your support, we will continue to flourish, bringing you timeless pieces that reflect strength, grace, and empowerment.

Join us in redefining fashion—one confident woman at a time.
